# CHANGES - Starshore

## v1.0.0 (first release)

- Scan every file in shared storage, show the list with sizes, and let you pick what to delete.
- Duplicates are matched by file content; one original per group always stays.
- Clean temp files, logs, caches, leftover APKs, empty folders, and gallery trash.
- Compress video with the hardware encoder, with a bitrate or target size per video.
- Microphone audio, internal audio, or both, muxed into the compressed file.
- Compare two videos by sliding, plus a 5 second preview before you compress.
- 8 color themes, your own background photo, vibration and click sound that can be turned off.
- The Batal button deletes the last word, press again for the one before it.
- The last 20 copied snippets can be pasted from the suggestion bar.
- Leftover folders of uninstalled apps, internal cache, and system logs when Shizuku or root is active.
- No internet permission, no ads, no data leaving the device.
