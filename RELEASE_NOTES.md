Starshore v1.0.0

First release: a junk cleaner and video compressor in one small app.

- You choose which files to delete. Nothing is ever deleted automatically.
- Duplicates are matched by file content, and one original per group always stays.
- Compression uses the hardware encoder, so it is fast and the APK stays small.
- No internet permission, no ads, nothing leaves your device.

Warning: deletion is permanent and there is no trash bin. Read the list first, and try it on a
folder you do not care about before a big clean. Cleaning inside other apps needs Shizuku or root.
SHA256SUMS.txt is attached so you can check the download. Android 7.0 or newer.
