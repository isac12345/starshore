# CHANGES - Starshore

## v1.0.0 (first release)

- Scan shared storage, show the list with sizes, and let you tick what to delete.
- Duplicates are matched by file content; one original per group always stays.
- Clean temp files, logs, thumbnails, cache folders, gallery trash, leftover APKs, empty folders.
- Compress video with the hardware encoder, by bitrate up to 90 percent of the original or by target size.
- Keep the original audio, shrink it to 64 kbps, or drop the audio track.
- Slide between original and compressed video, plus a 5 second preview before compressing.
- The Batal button stops a running compression and keeps what is already done.
- Clean app cache under Android/data and Android/media.
- With Shizuku or root: leftovers of uninstalled apps, internal cache, system logs, and TRIM.
- No internet permission, no ads, no data leaving the device.
